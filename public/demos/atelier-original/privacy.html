Privacy & demo details

Atelier Nocturne is a fictional portfolio website. It offers no hotel accommodation, does not process payments, cannot reserve rooms and does not collect visitor names, addresses, emails or payment data. The price estimator runs locally in your browser.

If you copy a generated example enquiry, it is copied to your clipboard only following your action. The site does not transmit the form selections to a server. External image hosts and Google Fonts may receive standard network requests when resources are loaded; review those providers' policies independently.

For a real commissioned website, the privacy statement would need to reflect the final hosting setup, analytics, forms, cookie choices and any connected booking systems.
