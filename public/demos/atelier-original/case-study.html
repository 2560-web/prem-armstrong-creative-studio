THE MAKING OF ATELIER NOCTURNE

Design with a reason.
Code with purpose.

A fictional hospitality concept built to demonstrate visual storytelling and practical front-end interactions.

Project status: Original independent portfolio demonstration. No paying hotel client, real room inventory, live booking system, payment processor, measured conversion results or fabricated testimonials.

The idea

Many hospitality websites need to balance atmosphere with usefulness. Visitors want to understand what makes a place special, compare different rooms and estimate the cost before making an enquiry. This prototype explores all three stages in one coherent experience.

What I built

  • Responsive editorial landing page with distinct room categories and intentional typography.
  • Functional room selector, date validation, guest count, optional extras and continuously updated illustrative price.
  • Accessible menu and modal with keyboard escape, focus management and reduced-motion support.
  • An enquiry summary that can be copied, rather than a fake successful reservation.
  • Separate project documentation and honest disclosure of demonstration limits.

Important technical limits

Room prices and options are hard-coded demonstration data. No availability API, backend, payment collection, email delivery or database is connected. The website deliberately does not collect personal details because its purpose is to demonstrate the interface, not accept real reservations.

The photographic imagery and web fonts are served by external providers and require a network connection. Production work would need licensed assets, performance testing and agreement on the business's booking provider.

How this translates to client work

The same techniques can support a restaurant, accommodation business or service provider: clear information architecture, responsive components, helpful forms and a visitor journey that turns interest into an actionable enquiry. A real client build would include agreed content, accessibility review, analytics requirements, privacy compliance and tested integrations.

Next validation steps

Test with real devices and browsers; check image licensing, optimize images, perform accessibility review and gather Lighthouse metrics. No unmeasured scores are claimed here.
